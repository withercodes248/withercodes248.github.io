Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy covers the "Withercodes uploader", a private tool that the owner of the Withercodes YouTube channel uses to upload videos to their own channel.

What the tool accesses

The tool uses Google OAuth with a single permission, youtube.upload, which lets it upload videos to the channel owner's YouTube account. It does not read, list or change any other data in the Google account or the YouTube channel.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner uses the tool. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from any other users.

Data storage

The OAuth credentials are stored privately by the channel owner (in a local file and in encrypted GitHub Actions secrets) and are used only to upload videos. No user data is sold, shared or sent to third parties.

Use of Google data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads go through the YouTube API Services, and viewers of the channel are covered by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Withercodes YouTube channel.
